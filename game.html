How to Play

Timed Mode:

• Answer before the timer runs out.
• Faster answers earn speed bonus points.
• Build streaks — every 5 correct gives a bonus!
• High scores are saved automatically.


Zen Mode:

• No timer, no pressure.
• Practice at your own pace.
• Type or tap numbers, then hit Enter.


Controls:

• Enter to submit
• Esc or Skip to pass
• On-screen numpad works too
